Before you start

The questions everyone asks.

What does the trial actually include?

Sixty days, every feature switched on, unlimited users. Not a cut down tier with the good parts locked.

Do I need a credit card?

No. A company name, your name, and an email. Nothing is charged and no card goes on file until you decide to keep going.

What happens to my data after 60 days?

Your account and your jobs stay put. Add a payment method to continue. We warn you inside the app well before the trial ends.

Can I put my whole crew in?

Yes, and you should. Users are unlimited on the trial and on Platform, so office, sales, PMs, and field all go in without changing the price.

Do I have to talk to anyone?

No. Start the trial and run a job. Book a demo if you would rather be walked through it, but nothing requires a call.

Can I start with example data?

Yes. Pick sample data and a few example jobs, estimates, and invoices are already there to click around. Clear it whenever you want.
