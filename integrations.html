Integrations

Connect the edges. Keep the job in one place.

FloBuildr is the system of record for the job. Payments, property data, and messaging plug into that record, so you don’t live in five apps.

QuickBooks Online

Connect your QuickBooks company and FloBuildr pushes invoices, bills, purchase orders, vendors, and receipts across, with jobs mapped to classes. Payments and expenses recorded in QuickBooks come back the other way, so the books and the jobs stay in step without double entry.

SRS supplier pricing

Live, account-specific pricing from your own SRS account, inside the estimate and the worksheet. Branches sort nearest first for the job address with yards you already buy from lifted to the top, and your choice sticks for that job. Mark materials Ordered and FloBuildr can submit the order through the supplier API, re-pricing at the branch before it sends, then tracking status back as they ship.

Online payments

Collect deposits and draws online. Customers see what’s owed in the portal and pay without calling the office.

Property & roof data

Enrich the job site with property context and aerial roof measurements. Always verify on site before ordering tight material counts.

Email & text notifications

Workflow notifications with clear usage. Keep campaigns and job texts off personal phones, without surprise carrier bills.

FloBuildr AI

An assistant on every screen that summarizes a job, reviews an estimate for anything missing, and answers how do I. A morning briefing ranks what needs attention by severity and links straight to the job. It answers from your own records and will not invent data. Included in Platform.

The point

Integrations shouldn’t replace your OS.

Most stacks fail because each tool owns a slice of the job. FloBuildr owns the Job Profile; payments, property data, and messaging plug into it.
