About

Built by people who run jobs.

FloBuildr started on a real job site, not in a software office.

The problem we lived.

FloBuildr was built by two partners. Jake builds the software. Bradley owns and runs MCA Construction & Roofing, a working GC and roofing operation with real crews on real jobs.

We did not study this problem. We paid for it. MCA was running a roofing tool for the roofing side and a GC tool for everything else, two logins, two bills, and neither one knew what the other was doing. Sales entered the bid, production rebuilt it from scratch, and the margin on a job was a question nobody could answer until the receipts were reconciled a month later. A spreadsheet held the seam together, badly.

Nobody was selling the thing we needed, so we built it in the business that needed it.

What we built.

One record, the Job Profile, that carries a job from the first call to the final payment. The lead, the bid, the active job, and the paid project are the same record at different stages, so nothing gets retyped and nothing falls in a gap between two systems.

One flat price, unlimited users, because the people who run the work should not cost extra to put in the system. Every hire on a per-seat tool is a reason not to give someone access. We were not going to build that into our own shop, and we are not going to sell it to yours.

MCA runs on it. Every part of FloBuildr has been used on a real job before it was offered to anyone else, and the things that did not work in the field got changed.

Who it is for.

Contractors and specialty operators who are paying for a stack of tools they half use. If you sell the work, schedule the crew, buy the materials, and collect the draw, the whole job belongs on one record, and you should not be buying four subscriptions to get there.

Roofing is where we go deepest, because it is where we started and where we still run jobs. It is not the whole of it. Remodelers, specialty trades, and light commercial all run the same way on the same record.

From the shop we built it in

“We’ve done about 20 jobs end to end, with around 30 people in the system, employees and subs. Estimates go out with e-sign so we’re not chasing PDFs. Workflows handle the follow-ups that used to eat the day. Customers get a portal and pay right there. Sales, office, and project management stay on the same job the whole way through. Even though we’re busier, I estimate we’re saving 30 to 40 hours a week across the company.”

Bradley McHugh, FloBuildr co-founder and owner of MCA Construction & Roofing
