About

Built by people who run jobs.

FloBuildr started on a real job site, not in a software office.

The problem we lived.

[DRAFT: confirm] FloBuildr was built by Jake, a developer, and Bradley, a working contractor, out of Bradley's own roofing and construction business. The tools they could buy forced a roofing app plus a separate GC app that never shared data, two logins, two bills, and a spreadsheet holding it together.

What we built instead.

One record, the Job Profile, that carries a job from first lead to final payment. One flat price, unlimited users, because the people who run the work should not cost extra to put in the system.

Who it is for.

[DRAFT: confirm] Contractors who run whole jobs, led by the roofing-heavy GCs we know best, because that is where we started.

From a shop running it

“We’ve done about 20 jobs end to end, with around 30 people in the system, employees and subs. Estimates go out with e-sign so we’re not chasing PDFs. Workflows handle the follow-ups that used to eat the day. Customers get a portal and pay right there. Sales, office, and project management stay on the same job the whole way through. Even though we’re busier, I estimate we’re saving 30 to 40 hours a week across the company.”

Bradley McHugh, Owner, MCA Construction & Roofing
