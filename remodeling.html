Stop rebuilding the winning bid

Remodelers usually lose the job in the handoff. The estimator wins with a detailed proposal. Production opens a new spreadsheet. The PM texts photos to a thread the office cannot search. FloBuildr pulls the accepted estimate into the worksheet with status, crew, and dates on every line.

Change orders are priced, signed, and added to the same job. Contract value and Budget vs Actual update together, so a tile upgrade is not an after-the-fact surprise.

One calendar for the company, not seven personal ones

Labor, materials, permits, punch, and tasks roll up to a company calendar. Crews see assignments and update progress from the field. Photos, daily logs, punch lists, and checklists attach to the job instead of vanishing into iMessage.
