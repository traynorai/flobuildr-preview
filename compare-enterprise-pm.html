  • We do not do heavy commercial. No RFIs, no submittals, no certified payroll, no AIA billing. If your work lives in that paperwork, an enterprise platform is the right call and we will say so.
  • Light commercial is still us. Missing the heavy commercial paperwork is not the same as not doing commercial work.
