Who it's for

Contractors who run whole jobs

If you sell the work, schedule the crew, buy the materials, and collect the draw. FloBuildr is built for you. If you only need a dispatch board for service tickets, there are cheaper tools.

Roofing & exterior

Live supplier pricing on the estimate, roof measurements when you need them, weather on the job dashboard, and change orders that don’t get lost between sales and production.

General remodelers

One Job Profile from first walkthrough through punch list. Sales, office, and crew look at the same worksheet, schedule, and budget.

Light commercial too

A commercial reroof, a strip mall unit, a small tenant fit-out. Residential and light commercial both run on FloBuildr. You only need a commercial platform when your work lives in RFIs, submittals, and AIA billing, and most contractors searching for one do not.

Specialty trades

Unlimited users so you can put estimators, PMs, and field leads on without counting seats. Workflows and SMS keep follow-ups off personal phones.

What changes when you switch

The handoffs stop being the product.

Most contractors do not fail for lack of software. They fail because every tool owns a slice of the job and nobody owns the whole story.

Sales stops rebuilding for production

The estimate that wins becomes the worksheet. Change orders stay on the same job instead of a parallel spreadsheet.

Margin shows up while you can still act

Budget vs Actual tracks committed and spent by line type before closeout, not after the final draw is already spent.

Homeowners stop texting for status

The portal shows progress, schedule, docs, and balances. You control the surface; they get a single place to look.

Adding people does not raise the bill

Unlimited seats means the software cost stays flat when you put estimators, PMs, and crew on the same system.

Field work lands on the same record

Photos, punch, checklists, and clock events attach to the job, not to a group chat or a separate time app.

One price, every feature

No “unlock scheduling later” tier. Estimating, production, portal, workflows, and SMS ship on Platform.

You’re a fit if…

You feel the multi-tool tax every week.

  • You re-enter winning bids into a second system (or a spreadsheet) to run production.
  • You’re afraid to add users because the software bill climbs with every hire.
  • You find out a job lost money after the final payment, not while the crew is still on site.
  • Homeowners text “how’s it going?” because they don’t have a single place to look.
  • Personal phones are the office SMS system and nobody can find the thread six months later.

You’re not a fit if…

You only need tickets.

  • Pure service / maintenance dispatch with no estimating or job costing.
  • Enterprise GC suites with dedicated IT and year-long implementations.
  • Shops that only need invoicing and don’t run a production plan.
  • Teams that want a dozen disconnected “best of breed” tools more than one job record.
