What only we do

Three things the rest of the market does not have.

The record takes the next step

Advance a material to Ordered and the purchase order opens, grouped by vendor. Mark it Delivered and the receipt logs against the job. Materials, labor, and equipment each carry their own lifecycle, and the money lands on Budget vs Actual as it happens. The worksheet does the work instead of reporting on it.

A briefing waiting for you every morning

FloBuildr ranks what needs attention by severity and links each item straight to the job. The assistant sits on every screen: summarize this job, review this estimate for anything missing, what should I focus on today. It answers from your own records and will not invent data it does not have.

Measure the roof from the satellite view

Trace the planes, set the pitch, and get roof area, squares, and squares with waste, plus eave, ridge, hip, valley, and rake lines. It saves to the property and every future job at that address starts with it. Certified auto-pitch reports are there when a job needs one.
