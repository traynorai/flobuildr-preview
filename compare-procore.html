  • We do not do heavy commercial. No RFIs, no submittals, no certified payroll, no AIA billing. If your work lives in that paperwork, Procore is the right call and we will say so.
  • Light commercial is still us. Missing the heavy commercial paperwork is not the same as not doing commercial work. Residential and light commercial both run on FloBuildr, including roofing GCs who take the occasional commercial reroof. [CONFIRM: Jake/Bradley, exact commercial scope Bradley’s shop does]

  • Right sized means the whole job, not less of it. Lead, estimate, worksheet, schedule, cost, invoice, and payment on one record.
