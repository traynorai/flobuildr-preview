Features/Customer portal

Give the customer a window into the job, not the office phone line

A secret portal link shares progress, documents, and billing on your terms, so homeowners stop calling for updates you already have on the Job Profile.

A secret link, not another login to manage

Homeowners get a private portal link tied to their job, no account creation, no password to reset. Progress, schedule, change orders, and invoices are all right where they left off the last time they checked.

Signed documents and photos land where the customer can find them

Contracts, change orders, and job photos post to the portal the moment they're finalized on the Job Profile. Nobody has to remember to forward a PDF or text a picture, it's already there.

Billing and online pay in the same place they check progress

Customers see what's due, deposits, draws, or the final invoice, and pay online without calling the office. Payments post back to the Job Profile so collected balance is never a guess.

You decide what the customer sees

Every section of the portal, photos, punch list, documents, messaging, is a visibility toggle you control per job. Share the win without exposing the parts of the Job Profile that are none of the customer's business.
