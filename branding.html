Brand

FloBuildr brand guidelines

Carbon surfaces, one amber accent, and lockups that stay sharp at any size. Use these assets as-is. Don’t redraw the mark or invent a second accent.

Name & voice

How we write it

Do

  • FloBuildr, capital F, capital B. Never “Flobuildr,” “Flo Buildr,” or “FB.”
  • Tagline: Every job. One platform. Two short sentences, periods included.
  • Talk about the Job Profile, the estimate that becomes the work plan, and live job cost.

Don’t

  • Don’t abbreviate the product name in customer-facing copy.
  • Don’t rewrite the tagline (“one place for every job,” “all-in-one,” etc.).
  • Don’t lean on purple gradients, cream+serif “AI defaults,” or invent a second brand accent for chrome/CTAs. (Product status chips are a separate categorical system. See below.)

Product UI

Categorical chips

Status, source, and type pills stay colorful so operators can scan calendar lists, pipeline, and boards. Do not collapse these to white/gray Bootstrap badges. Canonical map: app/src/utils/brandChips.js + BrandChip.

amber · on hold orange · in progress sky · sent / invoice violet · lead purple · job dates green · approved teal · order cyan · punch rose · dead red · overdue pink · time off slate · line items gray · draft

Do

  • Keep status/source chips colorful and distinct. Operators scan by hue.
  • Use light tinted fill + darker text so chips read on Ink and Paper.
  • Match calendar event borders to the same source hue as the chip.

Don’t

  • Don’t monochrome chips to white/gray Bootstrap badges for “brand purity.”
  • Don’t use Hi-vis Amber as a chip wash. Amber stays CTAs and the logo arm.
  • Don’t invent one-off chip hexes; change brandChips.js and update this page.

Usage

Clear rules

Do

  • Put fullcolor / graphite on paper or light UI; reverse / white on carbon, photos, or dark UI.
  • Keep clear space roughly the height of the “F” around the mark.
  • Download SVG from this page; prefer vector over flattened PNG when possible.
  • Let amber carry emphasis, buttons, the logo arm, sparse highlights.

Don’t

  • Don’t stretch, outline, recolor, drop-shadow, or place the mark on busy photos without a veil.
  • Don’t put reverse lockups on white, or fullcolor lockups on black.
  • Don’t invent a second accent (no steel blue, no purple).
  • Don’t use the app icon as a favicon, or the favicon as a marketing hero.
