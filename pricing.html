Questions

Before you start

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. You give us a company name, your name, and an email. Nothing is charged, and there is no card on file until you decide to keep going.

What happens after 60 days?

Your account and your data stay put. Add a payment method to continue on Platform. We warn you inside the app well before the trial ends, so the deadline never arrives as a surprise.

Is it genuinely unlimited users?

Yes. Office, estimators, project managers, and crew all count as zero. Price is set for the company, so putting more people on jobs never raises the bill.

What if we run past the included usage?

You get a warning as you approach the limit and can add a usage pack for that meter. Storage, email, SMS, property lookups, and AI are metered separately, so one heavy month in one place doesn’t cost you the others.

Can we get our data out?

Your estimates, invoices, and signed documents download from each job as PDFs. A full-account export is on the roadmap, ask us if you need it.

Do you help us move off our current tools?

Yes. Bring your line-item library, customer list, and open jobs, and we’ll walk the migration with you. Larger or multi-entity moves are handled on a Custom plan.
